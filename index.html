The Ridge

Six hundred and forty metres closer to the sky

Windy Ridge sits on the crest of the Tagaytay ridge, where the land drops away to the caldera and Taal Volcano rises from its lake. Mornings start at eighteen degrees with barako coffee on the terrace; by four the mist arrives, and the whole ridge goes quiet.

  • 640 mabove sea level, on the caldera rim
  • 24suites, every one facing the lake
  • 58 kmfrom Manila — under two hours by car

Accommodations

Three ways to wake above Taal

Every suite faces west toward the caldera. Rates are per night for two and include breakfast at Ember & Mist; 12% VAT and a 10% service charge are added at checkout.

Experiences

Curated for the ridge

Dining

Ember & Mist

Our restaurant sits on the west edge of the ridge, with a wood-fired hearth and a glass wall that opens to the caldera. The kitchen cooks from Cavite and Batangas — tawilis from the lake, beef from the lowlands, greens from the ridge garden — and pours barako grown twenty minutes down the road.

Wellness

The Ridge Spa

Five treatment rooms cut into the hillside, each with a window on the mist. The menu draws on hilot, the Filipino healing massage, and on the volcanic stone of the caldera below — warmed, oiled with coconut and pressed along the spine.
